# Privacy Policy — ModForge AI

Last updated: 1 November 2026

ModForge AI is a skills-based assistant plugin for helping users plan, create, inspect, and troubleshoot Minecraft Java mod projects. It does not operate its own hosted account system, analytics service, or independent backend for collecting user data.

## Information and processing

- Prompts, files, code, logs, and other content that you provide are processed by ChatGPT and the tools or connected services that you choose to use. Their handling is governed by the applicable provider terms and privacy policies.
- If you ask ModForge AI to use a connected GitHub account or GitHub Actions, project files and build information may be sent to or stored in the GitHub repository, workflow logs, and workflow artifacts that you selected. Repository visibility and GitHub retention/access settings apply.
- Generated source code, assets, logs, and build artifacts may contain information you supplied. Review them before publishing or sharing.
- ModForge AI does not independently request payment details or sell personal information.

## Retention and deletion

ModForge AI does not maintain a separate plugin-operated database of prompts or project files. Copies may remain in ChatGPT conversation history, connected services, GitHub commits, workflow logs, or artifacts according to those services' retention and deletion controls. Manage or delete those copies with the relevant provider.

## Children

Users must follow the age requirements and terms of ChatGPT and any connected services.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the plugin's behavior changes. For questions or privacy requests concerning this plugin, open an issue at https://github.com/p17371956-web/2/issues.

This policy describes ModForge AI itself; it does not replace the privacy policies of OpenAI, GitHub, or other services you connect.
